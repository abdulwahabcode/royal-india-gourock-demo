Our Story

A Family Kitchen Since 1972

Run by Matt & family, Royal India specialises in authentic West of Scotland-style curries — recipes refined over generations, tandoor fired daily, and a welcome that keeps Gourock coming back.

  • Dine-in above the Victoria Bar, takeaway & delivery
  • Set dining experience — four courses from £15 per guest
  • Vegetarian options across starters & mains
  • 20% off when you order on our own website
